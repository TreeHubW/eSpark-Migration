# POA vs GHI % gap (vs model)

**Units:** ratio  |  **Excel column:** AF  |  **Kind:** calculated  |  **Group:** Irradiation

Difference between the POA gap and the GHI gap. A way to see whether irradiation shortfalls are weather (both low) or something specific to the tilted plane (only POA low).
