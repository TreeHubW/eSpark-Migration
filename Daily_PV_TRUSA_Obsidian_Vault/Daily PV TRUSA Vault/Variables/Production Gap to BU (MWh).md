# Production Gap to BU [MWh]

**Symbol:** $\Delta E_{BU}$  |  **Units:** MWh  |  **Excel column:** T  |  **Kind:** calculated  |  **Group:** Production vs Budget

Production gap to budget: positive means the site beat the HQ budget, negative means a shortfall. This is the total that the gap bridge then explains.
